\# Project Statement: Library Management System



\## Problem Statement

Small libraries and college reading rooms often track books, members, and

issue/return records manually on paper or spreadsheets. This leads to lost

records, missed due dates, unpaid fines, and slow searching. There is a need

for a simple, reliable system that digitizes these operations.



\## Scope of the Project

\- A menu-driven, terminal-based Python application.

\- Management of books and members (add, view, update, delete, search).

\- Issuing and returning books with automatic due dates and fine calculation.

\- Reports (issued books, overdue books, member history) with CSV export.

\- Persistent storage using JSON files.

\- Out of scope: graphical interface, online payments, multi-branch support.



\## Target Users

\- Librarians managing daily operations.

\- College staff maintaining reading-room records.

\- Students and faculty who borrow books (as members in the system).



\## High-Level Features

1\. Book and Member Management (CRUD and search)

2\. Issue and Return Management (due dates, limits, fines)

3\. Reports and Analytics (overdue list, history, CSV export)

4\. Input validation and custom error handling

5\. Activity logging to a log file

6\. Librarian login with hashed passwords
